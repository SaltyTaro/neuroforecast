**NeuroForecast: day-7 trust triage for later changes in neuronal function.** Working project selected September 20, 2026 IST. The reserved validation was completed on September 21: **the learned forecast lost to carrying day 7 forward, and the reliability layer generalized.** The project scope narrowed accordingly; see [reserve results](neuroforecast_reserve_results.md).

A researcher running a chemical-exposure study can measure electrical activity before a neuronal culture has finished developing. The proposed tool uses day-7 measurements and matched controls to forecast the day-12 network-spike readout, then highlights conditions that may warrant closer observation or an additional functional measurement. It should display the measurements, the forecast, the simple comparator, and evidence about reliability together.

The first public-data implementation uses the [EPA rat cortical network formation assay](https://doi.org/10.23719/1503191). It has a defined time horizon, directly measured outcomes, and a benchmark that keeps both experiment dates and chemical identities out of training. The development cohort has 101 identified substances, 819 dose-condition cases, and 12 experiment-date groups. A further three dates containing 32 eligible substance identities formed the reserve, scored once on September 21. Dates are available batch-grouping units, not verified unrelated donor or litter identities.

The tool is `tools/neuroforecast_triage.py`. It accepts an audited CSV of day-7 wells and their zero-dose controls (day-5 rows optional, later days rejected) and returns three things per batch: a measurement-quality verdict, a per-condition table with the forecast, its 80% interval, the persistence and smaller-model comparators and a trust verdict, and two ranked lists at the validated 20% review budget. The second list, restricted to conditions still quiet at day 7, is where the forecast was validated to add information. It runs locally on CPU in seconds.

Two real examples ship with it, both from the sealed reserve: `reserve_20171011_usable_day5_day7.csv`, where the batch passes quality and 8 of 39 quiet conditions are surfaced, and `reserve_20171004_low_quality_day5_day7.csv`, where every plate had zero day-7 reference activity, the batch verdict recommends measuring day 12 directly, and all 16 surfaced conditions are declined. End-to-end tests check the tool reproduces the audited reserve forecasts, intervals, trust verdicts and quality flags exactly, and that removing day-5 rows changes none of them.

The contribution is a reproducible trust-triage and warning workflow with explicit timing, control quality, strong comparators, and failures. Early forecasting and MEA screening already have substantial prior work. Standard Extra Trees is an implementation choice. The team should not claim a new learning algorithm, a first neural forecast, a newly discovered chemical effect, or demonstrated chip performance.

On development, aggregate error was 15.2% lower than carrying the day-7 readout forward and 3.7% lower than the strongest smaller model, with uncertainty spanning no gain. The decisive test reversed the first of those comparisons: on the reserved batches persistence was more accurate. Keep both results visible.

The gate that produced that test required the following before interface work expanded:

1. **A defensible measurement target.** Quantify control and replicate variability on development data. Forty-two cases in the worst batch include a plate with a zero day-12 control median, which can inflate relative targets. Keep the frozen result intact; document any separately specified sensitivity analysis. A live rule can use only measurements available by day 7.
2. **A concrete warning decision.** Compare the selected forecaster, the small activity/coordination model, persistence, and a simple control/reference rule at a specified review budget or false-alert cost. Report misses and false alerts together. Lock the operating point on development data.
3. **An honest reliability estimate.** Evaluate uncertainty or abstention with chemical/date separation inside development. Nominal coverage without observed coverage and retained-case counts is insufficient. If reliability cannot be supported with this cohort, expose that limitation in the product.
4. **One locked evaluation on the reserve.** Freeze inputs, preprocessing, exclusions, baseline choices, operating point, metrics, and decision criteria before scoring its three dates. Report every batch. Do not recycle reserve outcomes into tuning or present three dates as broad external validation.

That gate is now closed. The reserved evaluation did not support a forecasting advantage over simple methods (persistence 0.6088 versus 0.7716), so the forecast claim is withdrawn and the supported measurement-review functions are retained, exactly as this brief required. The supported product is **trust triage**: a day-7 difficulty model that declines the conditions whose day-12 outcome cannot be predicted (14% of reserve cases, 51% of total error, improving persistence as well as the learned model), a day-7 control-quality display that identified the whole failing batch before any day-12 data existed, and a review-budget ranking that found 6 of 10 later large changes where the day-7 readout was uninformative and the naive rule found none. Persistence is a first-class predictor inside the tool, not only a baseline.

A five-minute demonstration should show one actual day-7 input, its control-quality display, the forecast alongside persistence and the smaller model, the reliability verdict (trusted or declined), a later observed result revealed for evaluation, and the 20171004 batch as the failure example. The current executable examples provide the inputs for that demonstration. Biological validation on living chips remains a future extension; this public-data project validates a workflow on chemical-exposed rat MEA cultures.
