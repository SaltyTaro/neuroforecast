Day-12 functional profile research preview

A second model, the batch-context transformer, reads every well of this batch at once: the controls, every plate and each chemical's whole dose series. It forecasts the day-12 change of all 17 readouts. Across 41 batches in cross-validation it beat carrying day 7 forward on all 36 profile targets and was about as accurate as 36 separately trained tree models. It has not been scored on a sealed test; the day-12 forecast above remains v2's.
