What this scores

Every usable condition of the 41 public EPA network-formation-assay batches (2,243 conditions, 243 chemicals), on the reference split: whole culture batches held out, and every chemical tested on them purged from training. Folds still mix years: trained only on the other period, the same model errs about 10% more (report §6.3). Labels are public, so a score here is self-reported: a shared yardstick, not a blind leaderboard. Upload a CSV with case_id and prediction (log2 day-12 network spikes relative to same-plate controls), and optionally low and high for an 80% interval. Case IDs, inputs and folds are in the repository (evaluation/v3/prepared/, evaluation/harness/folds.csv). Nothing is uploaded: scoring runs in this page.

Result

Error is date-macro MAE: absolute error averaged over doses within a chemical, then over chemicals within a batch, then equally over batches. Intervals are batch-paired bootstraps (10,000 resamples) of your error minus the baseline's; below zero means you are better. The Python scorer (tools/score_predictions.py) gives the same errors and batch counts; its bootstrap uses a different random stream, so intervals can differ in the third decimal.
